# MerchFlow AI

## An AI-Powered Prompt Library for Apparel Merchandising and Production Coordination

**Subject:** BUS4005 – Artificial Intelligence for Business  
**Assessment:** Assessment 1  
**Institution:** La Trobe University  

## 1. Project Overview

MerchFlow AI is a structured library of ten interconnected AI prompts designed to improve apparel merchandising, production coordination, operational reporting and management decision-making.

The project uses a hypothetical Bangladeshi garment manufacturer, Meridian Apparels Ltd., and synthetic business data to demonstrate practical applications of generative AI across the apparel merchandising lifecycle.

## 2. Business Problem

Apparel merchandising involves coordinating purchase orders, suppliers, material availability, sample approvals, production progress, quality inspections and shipment deadlines.

Manual information processing and fragmented communication can contribute to operational errors, delayed decisions, inconsistent reporting and delivery risks.

MerchFlow AI addresses these challenges through structured, evidence-based AI assistance while maintaining human decision-making authority.

## 4. Business Workflow

The ten prompts support the following operational workflow:

1. Purchase order validation
2. Requirements analysis and production scheduling
3. Supplier communication
4. Material readiness assessment
5. Sample approval tracking
6. Production performance monitoring
7. Quality issue assessment
8. Delivery risk escalation
9. Buyer status communication
10. Management performance reporting

The prompts support interconnected business activities, with human verification required before operational decisions are implemented.

## 5. Iterative Testing Methodology

Each prompt was developed and evaluated through a two-version testing process:

1. Develop the original Version 1 prompt.
2. Test Version 1 using Claude.
3. Critically evaluate the generated response.
4. Identify inaccuracies, limitations and operational risks.
5. Develop an improved Version 2 prompt.
6. Test Version 2 using Claude.
7. Compare both responses and justify the final selection.

Each prompt folder contains the original prompts, generated responses, critical evaluation and final version selection.

## 7. Expected Business Value

- Reduced manual information processing.
- Improved consistency of operational reporting.
- Earlier identification of production and delivery risks.
- Improved supplier and buyer communication.
- Stronger traceability and departmental accountability.
- Better evidence-based management decision-making.

These benefits represent anticipated business value rather than measured outcomes from a live organisational implementation.

## 8. Responsible AI and Risk Management

MerchFlow AI incorporates responsible AI principles through:

- Human verification and approval requirements.
- Evidence-based calculations and reporting.
- Restrictions on unsupported assumptions.
- Commercial confidentiality controls.
- Identification of missing and unverified information.
- Prevention of unauthorised operational and commercial commitments.

All AI-generated outputs require appropriate human review before business implementation.

## 11. Disclaimer

This project was developed for academic purposes. All companies, purchase orders and operational scenarios used in the testing process are fictional or synthetic.

The project demonstrates the potential application of generative AI in apparel merchandising and does not represent a validated commercial implementation.
